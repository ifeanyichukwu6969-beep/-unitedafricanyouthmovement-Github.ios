About UAYM

United Africa Youths Movement

Who We Are

The United Africa Youths Movement (UAYM) is a Pan-African youth organization dedicated to bringing together young Africans from across the continent and the diaspora. We believe that African youths are the key to Africa's future development, innovation, and prosperity.

Our Mission

To unite African youths globally, promote education, leadership, entrepreneurship, innovation, self-reliance, and cooperation for the advancement of Africa.

Our Vision

To build a strong, united, prosperous, and self-sufficient Africa led by empowered and visionary young people.

Our Core Values

  • Unity
  • Leadership
  • Integrity
  • Innovation
  • Education
  • Service to Africa

What We Do

UAYM supports youth empowerment through educational programs, entrepreneurship initiatives, leadership training, networking opportunities, community development projects, and Pan-African collaboration.
